Geometric Duality

Conformal Scaling of Black Hole Interiors is a long-form research program exploring whether open questions in cosmology have a single answer.

Geometric Duality treats the unresolved questions surrounding cosmic acceleration, horizons, singularities, and galaxy-scale dynamics as clues pointing toward a shared spacetime picture.

An evolving research program

The project develops this idea through a sequence of papers and working notes, beginning with a horizon-centered reading of cosmological geometry and extending toward its possible observational consequences.

The dedicated research site contains the current papers, open problems, supporting literature, and the working roadmap for the program.
